Quest controls

Left stick: move.
Right stick: snap turn.
Right trigger: draw chalk near a surface.
Right A: submit the circle.
Left X: make a lure noise.
Left Y: mute/unmute microphone.
Raise the left controller near your face: spyglass.

After starting, use the VR button in the bottom corner. Use physical controllers for this version.

Objective

Slowly draw a closed circle on the floor. Press A to activate it. Stand beyond it and make noise to lure The Echo across it. Escape through the green door.

Desktop: WASD move, mouse drag look, C draw, Enter submit, V spyglass, N lure, M mute, R restart.

Startup diagnostics are enabled. If a red error appears, copy its text or take a screenshot.
