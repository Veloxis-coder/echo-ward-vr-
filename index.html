Quest controllers

Left stick: move. Right stick: snap turn. Right trigger: draw chalk near the floor. Right A: submit rune. Left X: make a lure noise. Left Y: mute/unmute microphone. Raise the left controller near your face for the spyglass.

After starting, use A-Frame's VR button in the bottom corner. Use physical controllers, not optical hand tracking. A diagnostic panel in VR shows controller input.

Objective

Slowly draw a closed floor circle. Press A to activate it. Stand beyond it and use X or your voice to lure The Echo into it. Escape through the green door.

Desktop: WASD move, mouse drag look, C draw, Enter submit, V spyglass, N lure, M mute, R restart. Use HTTPS on Quest. Microphone capture and sharing are opt-in; only test with trusted people.

One arena, one simplified rune, procedural full bodies. Not photorealistic; elbows and legs are estimated. Multiplayer needs a separate signaling server. Performance and headset behavior need device testing.
