ECHO WARD

Two-player experimental WebXR survival room. Not a finished game.

Set the signaling URL in config.js before hosting on GitHub Pages.

Use headphones where practical. Your real voice may alert The Echo. Four-letter codes are invitations, not secure private accounts.

Quest controls

Enter VR after joining. Left stick: move. Right stick: snap turn. Right trigger: chalk (bring its tip close to a floor or wall). Right A: submit circle. Left X: lure/noise fallback. Raise left controller near your face to use the spyglass. Left Y: mute/unmute the shared microphone.

Desktop testing

WASD: move. Drag mouse: look. C: draw at the crosshair. Enter: submit circle. Hold V: spyglass. N: lure/noise. M: microphone toggle.

Draw a closed floor circle and submit it to banish The Echo. Find the green exit after a successful banishment.

Saved room bookmarks

Local device only; these are not accounts or a persistent friends service.
